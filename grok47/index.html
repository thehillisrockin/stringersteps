Step 6 of 7

Give them a real job

Paste this into the Ask anything box. Swap the [brackets] for a real listing, then press Enter. Every AI gets the same words at the same time.

Write a listing description for this home:
[3 bed, 2 bath, 1,800 sq ft ranch in Your Town. New kitchen, big fenced backyard, walk to downtown. Listed at $450,000.]

Rules:
- Under 150 words.
- Only use the facts I gave you. Don't make anything up.
- Don't use the words stunning, nestled or boasts.
- Follow fair housing rules: describe the home, not who should live there.

Then write a 2-sentence Instagram caption for it.
1
Grok 4.7 may take longer than the others. Let every answer finish.
2

Step 7 of 7

Pick a winner

Read the answers side by side and score each one on these four questions:

1
Did it follow every rule? Under 150 words, none of the banned words.
2
Is every fact right? It should only use what you gave it.
3
Did it describe the home, not the buyer?
4
Would you post it as is, or rewrite it?
Grok won? Keep using it here until it reaches the Grok app. Your usual AI won? Stick with it. Run this same test every time a new AI launches.
