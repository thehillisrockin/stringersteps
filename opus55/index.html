Listing Video
Let's make your video
Claude Opus 5.5

Make a Just Listed video with Claude

The new Claude makes motion graphics videos: moving text, smooth camera moves, music on the beat. Give it your listing photos and one prompt. You get an MP4 ready for Reels.

You need a paid Claude plan (Pro or higher), the Claude desktop app, and 3 to 8 listing photos. Your progress saves as you go.
(1)
Step 1 of 9

Put your photos in one folder

On your Desktop, make a new folder named Listing Video. Drag 3 to 8 listing photos into it.

1
To make the folder, right-click an empty spot on your Desktop and pick New Folder.
2
Claude only uses what’s in this folder, so use your real listing photos.
(2)
Step 2 of 9

Open the folder in Claude

Open the Claude app. Click the </> button at the top to switch to Code, then click New for a new session.

1 Code  2 New

⬇️ Get the Claude app claude.aiOpen →

Click the folder button above the message box, then Open folder… Click Desktop, then Listing Video, then Open.

1 Folder button  2 Open folder…

(3)
Step 3 of 9

Pick Opus 5.5 and Auto

Click the model name at the bottom right of the message box and pick Opus 5.5.

Then click the mode at the bottom left and pick Auto.

1
Opus 5.5 is the model behind the videos everyone is posting. New sessions often start on Sonnet, so check it every time.
2
Auto lets Claude work without stopping for your OK on every step. It still checks each command matches what you asked for.
3
No Auto in your menu? Leave it on Manual and click Allow each time Claude asks.
(4)
Step 4 of 9 · One time only

Install the HyperFrames plugin

HyperFrames turns what Claude designs into a real video file. Click the + at the bottom left, then Plugins, then Add plugin.

Type hyperframes in the search box and click Install.

1
It’s made by HeyGen and listed in Anthropic’s official plugin directory. Videos render on your computer, so there’s no account and no cost per video.
Use Codex, Cursor or Gemini CLI instead? HyperFrames works there too. Install it from hyperframes.heygen.com, then paste the same prompts from Steps 5 and 6.
(5)
Step 5 of 9 · One time only

Get your computer ready

Copy this, paste it in the message box, and press Enter. Claude checks for the two helper programs HyperFrames needs, installs any that are missing, and makes a test video.

Get my computer ready to make videos with the HyperFrames plugin.
1. Check that I have Node.js version 22 or newer and FFmpeg.
2. If either one is missing, install it for me in a way that does not need my computer password.
3. Test it by rendering a 3-second video that says "Ready" to an MP4.
When it works, reply in one line: Ready to make videos.
1
Leave it running until you see Ready to make videos. If Claude asks to run something, click Allow.
(6)
Step 6 of 9

Paste the video prompt

Copy this and paste it in the message box. Swap every [bracket] for your listing’s details, then press Enter.

Use the HyperFrames plugin to make a 15-second vertical video (1080x1920) for Instagram Reels that announces my new listing.

Photos: use only the photos in this folder. Open with the best one.
Listing details:
- JUST LISTED
- [Street address, City]
- [Price]
- [Beds · Baths · Square feet]
- [Your name · Your phone number]

Make it look like a pro real estate promo, not a slideshow: slow camera moves on every photo, bold clean text, cuts on the beat. Write your own original music with code (no downloaded songs) and match the motion to it. Do not invent any facts, features or text I did not give you.

First show me 3 different style directions, with one still picture of each. Build the one I pick.
1
Claude answers with 3 looks for your video, each with a still picture.
(7)
Step 7 of 9

Pick your favorite

Type the number you like and press Enter, like: Build number 2.

1
Claude builds the video and opens a preview. Leave it running until the preview opens.
(8)
Step 8 of 9

Change anything in plain words

Tell Claude what to change, one note at a time. It changes only that part and updates the preview.

Slow every zoom down by half.
Make the price bigger and hold it on screen 2 seconds longer.
Hard cut to the kitchen on the next big beat.
Make the music calmer and more upscale.
1
Happy with it already? Skip to Step 9.
(9)
Step 9 of 9

Save the MP4 and post it

Paste this and press Enter.

Render the final MP4 and open it.
1
The MP4 saves inside your Listing Video folder. To get it on your phone from a Mac, right-click the file, then Share, then AirDrop.
2
Post it as a Reel. It’s already the right size.
