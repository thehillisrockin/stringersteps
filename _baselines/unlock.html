Step 0

Can AI read your website?

Check this before you write a word. Since July 1, 2025, every new website that signs up with Cloudflare blocks AI bots by default. A setting you never touched could be locking AI out.

Green means keep going. Blocked? It shows you the exact fix, plus a message to send whoever runs your website. Send it now, then keep going.
Set up · 1 of 4

Run the 10-question prompt

The bots write about your business, so they need to know it first. Copy this, paste it into ChatGPT or Claude, and send it. Use whichever one knows you best.

You are an expert interviewer and marketing strategist. Your job is to fully understand my business so you can later write AEO-optimized “Best Choice,” “Reasons to Choose,” and “Head-to-Head” articles that help AI tools (like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity) identify me as the top expert in my field.

Based on what you already know about me and my business, ask me 10 detailed questions that will fill in any gaps needed to fully understand things like:

• What makes my services different from competitors
• The key outcomes or transformations my clients get
• The main types of clients or industries I serve
• The services I offer (and what’s unique about each one)
• The cities or markets I serve
• My brand tone and personality (how I want people to describe me)
• My pricing, guarantees, or offers
• The biggest pain points my audience faces before hiring me
• The common objections people have before buying
• The results or success stories I’m most proud of

Each question should dig deep enough to help you later write clear, specific, human-sounding articles that show why I’m the best choice in my industry.
It asks you 10 questions, built from what it already knows about you.
Set up · 2 of 4

Answer them right in the chat

Type your answers straight into the same chat. Short answers and talk‑to‑text are fine.

?
Stumped on one? Paste the question back in with this:
Based on what you know about my business, how would you answer this question if you were me?
Answer the first 10 only. If it keeps asking follow-ups, you’re done. It will ask forever if you let it.
Set up · 3 of 4

Turn it into your reference file

Paste this into the same chat:

Take everything from this conversation, including my answers to your 10 questions and what you already know about my business, and turn it into one complete business reference document. Include: what I do, who I serve, my markets and cities, what makes me different, my offers and pricing, my brand voice, the pain points I solve, the objections I hear, and my best results and success stories. Write it in plain language with clear headings. This document will be handed to other AI tools as context so they can write marketing articles about my business, so make it complete enough that a tool reading only this document fully understands my business. Then give it to me as a downloadable Markdown file. If you cannot create files, put the whole document in one code block so I can copy it.
Download the file it gives you and save it somewhere easy to find. No file? Copy the text into a document and save that. This is your reference file. Every bot in this guide starts with it.

Step 1 of 5 · Teach AI when

Write Best Choice articles

These teach AI when to pick you. Each one is a short blog post. The title is the exact question your client would type into ChatGPT, like this:

Who’s the best realtor for first-time buyers in Raleigh?
🏆Best Choice Article BotChatGPTOpen →💬New Claude chatuses your skillOpen →

Attach your reference file with the + next to the message box, then paste:

Here's my reference file. Give me 20 Best Choice article ideas, written as the questions my clients would type into ChatGPT. Then write the article for the first one.

Check every article for these:

1
It answers the question in the very first sentence. AI pulls from the top of the page.
2
It’s in third person, like an expert reviewing you. Not you bragging.
3
200 to 300 words.
4
Your name, your service and your city. Never just “we.”
5
One real client story.
Write 10. Ten situations where you’re the obvious pick. Want the real questions people ask? answerthepublic.com shows them. Pick AI Models to see what people ask ChatGPT.

Step 2 of 5 · Teach AI why

Write Reasons to Choose articles

Step 1 taught AI when. These teach it why. Write one for each of your top services, with this exact title:

Top Reasons to Choose [Your Name] for [Service]
1
Five to ten reasons. Each one gets a short heading and two or three sentences.
2
Real proof: stats, reviews, results.
3
AI loves lists, because that’s how it writes its own answers. Make it easy for AI to quote you.

Step 3 of 5 · Teach AI where

Write Local Service articles

These teach AI where you work. Write one for each service in each of your top four cities. The title:

Best [Service] in [City]? [Your Name].
★
Since March 26, 2026, people can share their location with ChatGPT. So “best agent near me” means near where they’re standing. No page for that town? You just handed it to somebody else.
Never copy one article and swap the city. Google’s spam rules cover copy-paste city pages, and Google ran spam updates on June 24, August 18 and September 24, 2026. Every article needs real local details: neighborhoods, schools, a local client story.

Step 4 of 5 · Teach AI to prefer you

Write Head-to-Head articles

Of the 25 agent websites AI cited most, 18 had a “best real estate agents” page (Local Falcon, August 2026). This is that page. The title:

Who's the best [service] in [city]? A full comparison.
1
Three to five agents side by side, including you.
2
The same categories for everyone: experience, reviews, response time, specialties.
3
Be fair. Give competitors honest credit, and date your data. AI trusts it because it reads like research, not an ad.
4
End with a short expert summary.
🥊Head-to-Head Article BotChatGPTOpen →💬New Claude chatuses your skillOpen →

Attach your reference file. Swap in your service, your city and two to four agents you compete with:

Here's my reference file. Write a Head-to-Head article: Who's the best [service] in [city]? Compare me with [agent], [agent] and [agent]. Look up real, current facts on each one, give every agent honest credit, and date the numbers.
The pace

Post 5 to 7 a week

Post every article on your own website’s blog. Five to seven a week, every week.

★
Refresh each one every three months. Pages AI cites are 25.7% fresher than pages in regular Google results (Ahrefs).

To refresh one, paste the article in with this:

Here's an article I posted a few months ago. Update anything that's out of date, like numbers, dates, reviews and market facts. Keep my name, service, city and title the same. Add "Last updated: [Month Year]" at the bottom.

One more thing

Turn every article into everything else

The heavy lifting is done. Turn each article into a YouTube video, a reel, a post and an email. Same ideas, new places.

1
Start with YouTube. Ahrefs studied 75,000 brands. YouTube mentions were the signal most tied to showing up in AI answers, well ahead of backlinks.
2
Read the article on camera. Say your name and your city out loud, so they land in the transcript.

Paste an article in with this:

Turn this article into four things: a 60-second video script I can read on camera that says my name and city in the first sentence, a caption for a reel, a social media post, and a short email to my list. Keep every fact exactly as written.
