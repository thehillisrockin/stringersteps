Your AEO Workshop homework

Two quick jobs before we build your articles together at the workshop. It takes 15 to 20 minutes, tops.

You’ll build your business reference file and grab your article tools, so we hit the ground running on workshop day.

Skipping the homework is fine, that’s up to you. But you can’t be the business Phil builds live on the call without it. We spin a wheel. Be ready.
(1)
Step 1 of 5

Run the 10-question prompt

Copy this whole prompt, paste it into ChatGPT or Claude, and send it. Use whichever one knows your business best.

It will ask you 10 questions, built from what it already knows about you.
(2)
Step 2 of 5

Answer them right in the chat

Type your answers straight into the same conversation. No document needed yet.

Short answers are fine. Talk-to-text is fine. It does not have to be pretty.
?
Stumped on one? Paste the question back in along with this:
Based on what you know about my business, how would you answer this question if you were me?
Answer the first 10 only. If it keeps asking follow-up questions, you’re done. It will ask forever if you let it.
(3)
Step 3 of 5

Turn it into your reference file

Now have it build the file we’ll use at the workshop. Paste this in the same chat:

Take everything from this conversation, including my answers to your 10 questions and what you already know about my business, and turn it into one complete business reference document. Include: what I do, who I serve, my markets and cities, what makes me different, my offers and pricing, my brand voice, the pain points I solve, the objections I hear, and my best results and success stories. Write it in plain language with clear headings. This document will be handed to other AI tools as context so they can write marketing articles about my business, so make it complete enough that a tool reading only this document fully understands my business. Then give it to me as a downloadable Markdown file. If you cannot create files, put the whole document in one code block so I can copy it.
Download the file it gives you (or copy it into a Google Doc) and save it somewhere easy to find. That’s your reference file. You’ll drag it into every AI conversation that writes about your business.
