01
Before you write a word

Can AI even read your site?

Some websites turn AI robots away at the door. If yours is one of them, every article you write today is invisible. Check first. It takes ten seconds.

Green means keep going. If it says you are blocked or your host is turning AI away, the tool gives you the exact fix and a message to send whoever runs your website. Send it now so it is being fixed while you write today.
Bookmark this one on its own: stringersteps.com/botcheck. Re-run it any time you change hosts, switch website builders, or add a security plugin. Those three things flip a site to blocked overnight.
02
Your reference file

Give the bots your business

The four writing bots know exactly how to write an AEO article. They know nothing about you. Your reference file is what turns a generic article into an article about you, in your market.

If you did the homework, you already have it. Open it now and keep it handy, because you will upload it four separate times today.

Do not have it yet? Do it right now, before step 3. It is one prompt and about five minutes: stringersteps.com/aeohomework. You cannot write the articles without it.
Save it as plain text. Export it as .txt or .md. AI reads those cleanest. A Pages file will fail silently and the bot will write about somebody else entirely. A Google Doc or Word file works, plain text is safest.
Put your name and market in the first two lines. Something like "My name is Gwen Wang. I am a realtor in the Bay Area." It removes any doubt about who the article should recommend.
03
Read once, applies to all 100

The rules for every article

These are the same for all four article types. Learn them here and the next four steps are just copy and paste.

1
Third person, always. An expert writing about you, never you writing about yourself. First person reads as self-promotion and AI discounts it. The bots do this automatically. Do not undo it.
2
No call to action. No phone number, no "visit my site," no "call me today." One CTA and AI treats the whole article as an ad.
3
Short. 200 to 300 words. These are not blog posts. AI wants clarity and pattern, not an essay.
4
The title is the exact question someone types. Match the prompt as closely as you can. That match is what gets you pulled into the answer.
5
Four articles at a time, maximum. Ask for five or more and the bot starts cutting corners.
6
Post three to seven a week. Never all at once. Five to seven is the sweet spot. Dumping 100 posts in one day looks like spam.
7
One article per post. Each one is its own page on your website. Never combine them.
Three words you will hear all day. Crawling is AI reading your page. Indexing is AI filing it away. Ranking is AI deciding where you land when it answers someone. Everything today is about earning all three.

These tell AI when to recommend you. Each one answers a real question people are typing, and the title is that question word for word.

Step 3 of 4 · 40 articles

Local service articles

Now you tell AI where you work. Your 10 services across 4 areas is 40 articles. It is the biggest step and the one that pays the most.

Why this one matters more than it used to. ChatGPT now uses a person's location when they ask. Someone standing in your town asking who the best is nearby is the exact moment these articles win.
1
Add four areas as columns to the right of your services sheet.
Four, not ten. More areas do not hurt, they just stop paying for the effort. Four is the sweet spot. Pick the four people actually type: cities, neighborhoods, a well-known landmark area, or the regional name everyone uses.

Step 4 of 4 · 40 articles

Head to head articles

This is the one that makes people squirm. You are going to compare yourself directly to the top names in your market, and it is the strongest signal on the list.

Read this before you skip it. AI is already recommending those people. The only question is whether you are in the conversation. You are not calling anyone bad. You are showing where you are the better fit for a certain kind of client.
1
Add two to four competitors to your sheet. With you, that makes three to five in each comparison.
Pick the big names, not the easy ones. Choose whoever AI already recommends in your market. Being compared to them is how you get listed beside them. There is always an angle: you answer your own phone, you give undivided attention, you know one neighborhood cold.
2
Open the Head to Head bot and upload your reference file.

08
Housekeeping

Where these go

Each article is its own post on your own website, in the blog or articles section.

No website of your own? Buy your name as a domain and put a simple site on it. If your brokerage controls your current site and will not let you post, this is worth doing anyway. You want a place nobody else controls.
Do not want clients reading these? Fair, they are written about you in the third person. Move the blog link to the footer or take it out of your menu completely. AI still reads every page. It does not need a link in your navigation, and no human ever has to see them.
Skip the images. They do not help AEO at all. AI is reading the words. Add one only if your website forces you to.
09
Off your website

Claim your platforms

AI does not only read your site. It checks whether the rest of the internet agrees you exist. Claim these and fill out every field.

1
Bing Places. The one almost nobody does, and the biggest single win here. Do this one first. bingplaces.com
2
Google Business Profile. Most people have it. Make sure every section is filled in, not just the name. business.google.com
3
Foursquare. Sounds dead. Is not. AI pulls a surprising amount from it. foursquare.com
4
LinkedIn company page and Better Business Bureau.
5
Your industry directory. For real estate that is your Zillow agent profile. Whatever the big one is in your field, claim it.
6
Local directories. Chamber of commerce, neighborhood associations, local news sites.
Identical everywhere. Same name, same spelling, same phone number, same description. When those do not match across sites, AI stops trusting all of them. Write your About section once and paste it everywhere.
10
Ten minutes, one time

Google Search Console

This tells Google your website is yours and asks it to read your pages properly. Everything you post gets found faster.

1
Go to search.google.com/search-console and click Start now.
2
Add your domain and follow the steps to verify you own it.
3
Submit your sitemap if your website has one. Most website builders make one automatically at /sitemap.xml.
Stuck on the verification part? Whoever set up your website can do it in about two minutes.
11
Sounds technical. Is not.

Add schema to your pages

Schema is a small piece of code that spells out what a page is about, so AI does not have to guess. You do not have to know how to write it. AI writes it for you.

1
Article schema on each article, so it reads as a real piece of writing.
2
FAQ schema on any page that answers questions.
3
Speakable schema tells voice assistants which parts to read out loud. Almost nobody is doing this one, which is exactly why it is worth doing.
1
Paste this into ChatGPT or Claude with one of your articles.
Write the JSON-LD schema markup for the article below. Include Article schema, FAQPage schema for any questions it answers, and speakable schema marking the sections a voice assistant should read aloud. Give it to me as one block of code I can paste into the page, and tell me exactly where it goes.

Here is the article:
[paste your article]
2
Paste the code it gives you into that page. In WordPress, use a code block or a plugin field. On other builders, look for a custom code or head section.
If someone helps with your website, forward them the code and ask them to add it. It is a five-minute job for them.
12
Where AI gets its opinions

Get into the Reddit threads

AI leans on Reddit heavily when it decides who to recommend. Getting your name into those threads is quick and most people never think to do it.

1
Search Google for Reddit threads in your market. Paste this into Google and swap in your details.
reddit.com best [your job] in [your area]
2
Open the threads and leave a genuinely useful comment with your name and the area you serve.
Old threads still count. A two-year-old thread will not bring you that client, and that is not the point. AI reads the whole thread. You want your name showing up across a lot of them.
3
Be helpful, not promotional. Answer the actual question. Reddit removes anything that reads like an advertisement, and so does AI.
13
Free multiplier

Use the same 100 everywhere

You just found out exactly what people ask in your market and answered all of it. That is a content calendar. AI reads video transcripts, captions and posts too.

1
YouTube. One video per question from step 1. Use the question as the video title. Transcripts get read.
2
Short form. Reels, TikTok, Shorts. Answer one question in 45 seconds.
3
Carousels. Turn a reasons-to-choose article into slides.
4
Email. Your list gets the same answers, one at a time.
Start with step 1. Those are the highest-volume questions in your market. Everything else is a bonus.
